## The challenge

Harbor Health's patients juggled three different portals, phone queues and paper reminders. A third of no-shows were people who had simply forgotten — and every one cost a clinic slot.

## What we did

We interviewed patients and front-desk staff across six clinics, then prototyped the booking flow until a first-time user could book in under a minute. The app was built in React Native from one codebase, with an offline-first appointment list and accessible, large-type layouts for older patients.

A thin Node.js API sits in front of the clinics' existing scheduling system, so nothing had to be replaced on day one.

## The outcome

Missed appointments dropped by 38% within four months. The app holds a 4.8-star rating and reached 120,000 patients in its first year.
