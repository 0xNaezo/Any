## The challenge

Fieldnote's founders had a sharp insight — product teams lose most of what they learn from customer interviews — and a fundraising window that closed in three months.

## What we did

In the first week we cut the planned scope by half, keeping only what proved the core idea: capture, tag and search interview notes across a team. Design and engineering ran in parallel, with a working build on staging from week two and a demo every Friday.

## The outcome

Fieldnote launched in eleven weeks, on the date promised in our first call, and raised a $2.4M seed round a month later. We still work with them as their core engineering team.
